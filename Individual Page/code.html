About the Role

As a Senior Strategy Consultant at McKinsey & Company, you will work on the most challenging problems facing the world's leading organizations. You will join a team of exceptional professionals who are passionate about delivering breakthrough impact.

You will lead workstreams, manage complex analytical projects, and build strong relationships with clients ranging from Fortune 500 CEOs to government leaders. This role requires a unique blend of analytical rigor, creative problem solving, and empathetic leadership.
